The **Studio** plan now comes with a GPU carrying **16 GB** of VRAM, up from 12 GB. The price stays at Rp1.2 million per month.

The increase answers requests from users running AI models and large 3D scenes, where VRAM capacity is usually the first thing to run out.

Active Studio customers are upgraded automatically on their next billing cycle, with nothing to do on their side.
