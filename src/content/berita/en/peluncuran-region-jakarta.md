---
title: "Xyverse Opens the Jakarta Region"
desc: "The new region cuts latency by up to 40% for users on Java and nearby islands."
date: 2026-09-12
tag: "Infrastructure"
lang: "en"
---

From today, every Xyverse Cloud PC plan can be deployed in the **Jakarta region**, alongside the Singapore region that was already available.

Internal testing shows average latency falling **from 32 ms to 19 ms** for users on Java. The difference is clearly felt in interactive work such as cloud gaming and video editing.

Existing customers can move their instance to the new region at no extra cost. Just contact the support team; migration takes around 30 minutes with no data loss.

There is no price change for the new region.
