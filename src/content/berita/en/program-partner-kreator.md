Xyverse is opening its **Partner Programme** to content creators, developer communities, and educational institutions.

Partners earn recurring commission on every customer they refer, a dedicated discount code for their audience, and free infrastructure credit for producing content.

Applications open by email from the contact page. We review every submission within three working days.
