Review rejections usually come down to small things that slipped through. Here is the list we run through on every release.

## Assets & metadata

1. An adaptive 512×512 PNG icon with no alpha channel
2. A 1024×500 feature graphic
3. At least four screenshots per screen size
4. A short description of no more than 80 characters
5. A long description carrying the relevant keywords

## Legal & policy

6. A privacy policy published at a publicly reachable URL
7. The data safety form filled in honestly and completely
8. Sensitive permission declarations with a stated reason for each

## Technical

9. A target SDK that meets the latest requirement
10. An app bundle signed with the correct upload key
11. A crash-free rate above 99% in internal testing
12. Testing on low-RAM devices and slow connections

Miss even one and you can expect several extra days of waiting.
