These two methods are the ones most often used to reach a Cloud PC. Each is strong in a different area.

## RDP — the workhorse for getting work done

Microsoft's Remote Desktop Protocol is very efficient for text and document work. Bandwidth use is low, text is crisp, and multiple monitors are supported.

Its weakness shows with moving content: video and animation tend to stutter.

## Parsec — the workhorse for visuals

Parsec was designed for game streaming and uses hardware encoding on the GPU. The result is very low latency, often under 20 ms on a good connection.

It suits cloud gaming, video editing, and 3D work. The one requirement: the instance must have a GPU.

## Conclusion

Use **RDP** for office work and coding. Use **Parsec** when you need smooth visuals. Every Xyverse plan supports both, so you can switch whenever your needs change.
