## 3. What a valid report must contain

A report must include every one of the following:

1. **Reporter identity** — full name, address, email, and a contactable phone number.
2. **Identification of the work** — a description of the copyrighted work said to be infringed, with a link to the original where possible.
3. **Location of the material** — the full URL or IP address where the reported material sits. We cannot process a report without a specific location.
4. **Evidence of ownership** — a copyright registration number, a first publication date, or other adequate evidence.
5. **Good-faith statement** — a statement that the use of the material is not authorised by the rights holder, their agent, or the law.
6. **Statement of accuracy** — a statement that the information in the report is accurate and that, under penalty of perjury, the reporter is authorised to act for the rights holder.
7. **Signature** — a physical or electronic signature of the rights holder or their agent.

Incomplete reports are returned with an explanation of what is missing.

## 4. How we handle a report

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–24 working hours | The report is received and checked for completeness |
| 1–2 working days | The customer concerned is notified and asked to respond |
| 3–5 working days | Where there is no response, access to the material is disabled |
| Afterwards | The reporter is told the outcome |

For very clear-cut, high-impact cases we may disable access sooner.

## 5. Counter-notices

A customer whose material has been disabled may file a counter-notice containing:

1. Full identity and contact details.
2. Identification of the disabled material and where it previously sat.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the takedown resulted from a mistake or misidentification.
4. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the competent court.
5. A signature.

Send it to [xycdigital@gmail.com](mailto:xycdigital@gmail.com). We will pass the counter-notice to the reporter. If the reporter does not pursue legal action within **10 working days**, the material may be restored.

## 6. Repeat infringers

An account that receives **three valid reports** that are not successfully countered will be terminated permanently without refund.

## 7. Wrongful reports

Submitting a false report can create legal liability for any resulting loss, including legal costs. Please be sure you really are the rights holder or their agent before reporting.

## 8. Reporter privacy

The content of a report, including the reporter's identity, is passed to the customer reported, because that is necessary for them to respond fairly. If you object to this, say so and we will look for a reasonable middle ground.
