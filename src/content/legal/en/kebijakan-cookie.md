## 1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file your browser stores when you visit a site. Cookies help a site remember preferences and keep a session running.

Alongside cookies, this site also uses **browser local storage** (localStorage), which works similarly but is never sent to the server.

## 2. The cookies we use

### 2.1 Strictly necessary

Without these the site does not work properly. No consent is required.

| Name | Type | Lifetime | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xy-theme` | localStorage | Permanent | Stores your light or dark mode choice |
| `xy-session` | Cookie | Session | Keeps you signed in to the customer panel |
| `xy-csrf` | Cookie | Session | Protects forms against request forgery |

### 2.2 Functional

These improve comfort and can be turned off.

| Name | Type | Lifetime | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `xy-region` | localStorage | 90 days | Remembers the region you last selected |
| `xy-dismissed` | localStorage | 30 days | Remembers notifications you have closed |

### 2.3 Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics: **no cross-site cookies, no device fingerprinting, and all data collected in aggregate**. We cannot identify an individual visitor from it.

### 2.4 Advertising

**None.** This site sets no advertising cookies and carries no tracking pixels from any ad network.

## 3. Third-party cookies

Certain embedded content may set its own cookies:

| Service | When it appears | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Where a video is embedded | policies.google.com/privacy |
| Google Maps | On the contact page, only once clicked | policies.google.com/privacy |

We load this kind of content lazily, so third-party cookies are only set once you interact with it.

## 4. Controlling cookies

### Through your browser

- **Chrome** — Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox** — Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari** — Preferences › Privacy › Manage Website Data
- **Edge** — Settings › Cookies and site permissions

### What turning them off does

Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break signing in and form submission. Blocking functional cookies simply means preferences are not remembered.

## 5. Do Not Track signals

Where a browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we honour it by disabling all analytics collection automatically.

## 6. Changes

If we add a new kind of cookie, the tables above are updated and the date at the top of this page changes with them.
