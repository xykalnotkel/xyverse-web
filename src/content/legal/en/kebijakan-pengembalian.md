## 1. The 7-day guarantee

**New** Cloud PC customers may request a full refund within **7 calendar days** of their first Instance going live, with no explanation needed.

Conditions:

- It applies once per customer, not per Instance.
- Bandwidth use has not exceeded 100 GB.
- There has been no breach of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/en/legal/kebijakan-penggunaan).
- The request is made before day 8.

## 2. After the 7 days

### 2.1 Ordinary cancellation

A subscription can be stopped at any time. The service runs to the end of the period already paid for. **The remainder of the current period is not refunded**, except as set out below.

### 2.2 Disruption on our side

Where monthly uptime falls short of the SLA commitment, you are entitled to service credit under the [Service Level Agreement](/en/legal/sla). Credit is applied automatically once a claim is approved.

### 2.3 Where we cannot deliver the service

If we are unable to provide an Instance to the specification ordered, all funds are returned in full, including any setup fee.

### 2.4 Billing errors

Double charges or incorrect amounts are refunded in full within 5 working days of verification.

## 3. What cannot be refunded

| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Setup fees for a custom configuration | The work has already been done |
| Third-party software licences | Already activated in your name |
| A dedicated IP address already allocated | The resource has been reserved |
| Elapsed time on a discounted annual plan | The discount was given for the period commitment |
| An account terminated for a breach | Per the Terms of Service |
| Promotional credit or vouchers | Not money you paid in |

## 4. Annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions receive a discount in return for a period commitment. Cancelling part-way through is calculated as follows:

1. The time already used is recalculated at the normal monthly price.
2. The difference between what you paid and that recalculation is refunded.
3. If the result is zero or negative, there is no refund and no additional charge.

## 5. App and software development services

Project work follows the payment terms written into the contract:

| Stage | Share | Refund status |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 30% | Non-refundable once research and design have begun |
| Mid-project | 40% | Pro-rated against work not yet carried out |
| Handover | 30% | Paid on acceptance of the deliverable |

If Xyverse stops a project for reasons not attributable to the Client, all funds for work not yet carried out are refunded in full.

## 6. How to apply

1. Email [xycdigital@gmail.com](mailto:xycdigital@gmail.com) with the subject **"Refund Request"**.
2. Include your account email, the invoice number, and a brief reason.
3. We respond within **2 working days**.
4. If approved, funds are sent within **7–14 working days** to the original payment method.

For bank transfers, include an account holder name matching your account details.

## 7. Charges incurred

Administrative fees from the payment provider, where any apply, are borne by Xyverse for refunds caused by our error, and by the Customer for cancellation by choice.

## 8. Disputes

If a request is declined and you disagree, ask for a review at [xycdigital@gmail.com](mailto:xycdigital@gmail.com). We favour resolving matters by discussion before any formal route.
