## 1. The spirit of this policy

Computing resources are shared. This policy makes sure one customer never spoils things for another, and keeps our network's reputation intact so our IP addresses stay off blocklists.

We take a human approach: where a problem is detected we contact you first before acting, except for serious breaches that pose real danger.

## 2. Permitted use

- Office work, design, and software development
- 3D rendering, video processing, and other heavy computation
- Development, testing, and continuous integration servers
- Lawful personal bots or automation
- Learning, research, and technical experimentation
- Running databases and internal team services

## 3. What is prohibited

### 3.1 Illegal activity

Anything that breaks the law of the Republic of Indonesia, including online fraud, unlicensed gambling, trade in prohibited goods, and child exploitation material. A breach in this category results in **immediate termination without warning and without refund**, and is reported to the authorities.

### 3.2 Cryptocurrency mining

Mining with CPU or GPU is not permitted on regular plans because it loads shared resources continuously. If you need it, contact us about a dedicated-allocation plan.

### 3.3 Network attacks and abuse

- Denial of service attacks (DoS/DDoS) of any kind
- Port or vulnerability scanning against systems belonging to others
- Attempts to gain unauthorised access to any system
- IP address spoofing or network traffic manipulation
- Running open proxies or relays that are then abused

### 3.4 Spam and email abuse

- Sending bulk email without the recipients' consent
- Harvesting email addresses
- Running an open SMTP relay

Port 25 is closed by default. It can be opened once the need has been verified.

### 3.5 Malicious software

Creating, storing, or distributing viruses, ransomware, botnets, or credential-stealing tools.

### 3.6 Copyright infringement

Storing or sharing copyrighted work without the owner's permission. See the [DMCA Policy](/en/legal/dmca).

### 3.7 Excessive account sharing

One Instance is intended for one user unless your plan says otherwise. Reselling access without a formal reseller agreement is not permitted.

## 4. Resource limits

| Resource | Reasonable limit | Note |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | 100% for at most 6 hours in a row | Sustained load needs a dedicated plan |
| Bandwidth | Per plan quota | Overage is billed per GB |
| Disk operations | 3,000 IOPS on average | Short bursts are fine |
| Outbound connections | 500 concurrent | More than that needs approval |
| Snapshots | Per plan | Old snapshots are removed automatically |

These figures are guidance, not automatic cut-offs. We look at your usage pattern as a whole before acting.

## 5. Enforcement steps

| Step | Situation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A first minor breach | Notification email, 7 days to put it right |
| 2 | No correction | Temporary resource throttling |
| 3 | Repeated breach | Instance suspended, data kept safe |
| 4 | Serious or badly repeated breach | Permanent account termination |

For an active threat, such as an attack leaving your Instance, we may isolate the network immediately and notify you afterwards.

## 6. Reporting abuse

If you find abuse originating from our network, report it to [xycdigital@gmail.com](mailto:xycdigital@gmail.com) with the IP address, a timestamp including its time zone, and the relevant log excerpt.

We respond to abuse reports within **24 hours**.

## 7. Filing an objection

If you believe an enforcement action was wrong, send an objection to [xycdigital@gmail.com](mailto:xycdigital@gmail.com) within 14 days. We will review it and reply within 5 working days.
