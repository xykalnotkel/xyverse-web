## 1. Introduction

This policy explains how Xyverse treats the personal data of customers and site visitors. We are committed to complying with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection as well as international best practice.

**Data controller:** Xyverse, Tanak Beaq, Central Lombok, West Nusa Tenggara 83562, Indonesia.

## 2. The data we collect

### 2.1 Data you give us directly

| Type of data | Example | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name, company name | Creating an account and billing |
| Contact | Email, phone number | Service communication and support |
| Payment | Method and transaction history | Processing invoices |
| Message content | Messages sent through the contact form | Answering your questions |

We do **not** store full credit card numbers. Payment processing is handled by a PCI-DSS certified gateway provider.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

- IP address and approximate city-level location
- Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution
- Pages visited and how long you spent on them
- Technical logs: connection time, session length, and Instance bandwidth volume

### 2.3 Data we do NOT collect

We do **not** monitor, scan, or copy the contents of files inside a Customer's Cloud PC. We do not record the screen, log keystrokes, or watch activity inside a remote session.

## 3. Legal basis for processing

| Basis | Where it applies |
|---|---|
| Performance of a contract | Delivering the service you bought |
| Legal obligation | Keeping tax records and responding to official orders |
| Legitimate interest | System security, abuse prevention, service improvement |
| Consent | Marketing newsletters and analytics cookies |

Consent can be withdrawn at any time without affecting the validity of earlier processing.

## 4. How we use data

1. To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
2. To process payments and issue invoices.
3. To send essential service notices (these cannot be opted out of while an account is active).
4. To provide technical support at the Customer's request.
5. To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
6. To share news of new products, only where you have agreed to it.

## 5. Sharing data with third parties

We do **not sell personal data**. Data is shared only with:

- **Infrastructure providers** — the data centres where Instances run.
- **Payment processors** — to complete transactions.
- **Transactional email providers** — to send service notifications.
- **Law enforcement** — only under an official order valid under Indonesian law.

Every third party is bound by a confidentiality agreement and may use the data only for the purpose we specify.

## 6. Storage and data location

Customer Instances run in the region the Customer chose, either Singapore or Jakarta. Account and billing data is held on servers in Indonesia.

| Type of data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Active account data | For as long as the account is active |
| Data after account closure | 30 days, then deleted |
| Financial records | 10 years, per tax rules |
| Technical logs | 90 days |
| Instance snapshots | Per plan, up to 30 days |

## 7. Security

The protections we apply:

- TLS 1.3 encryption for all data traffic
- Disk-level storage encryption
- Full isolation between customer Instances
- Two-step authentication on the panel and for administrative access
- Audit logging of every administrative access
- Regular security reviews of the infrastructure

No system is entirely impregnable. Should an incident occur that risks harming customers, we will give notice within **3 × 24 hours at most** of becoming aware of it, as the Personal Data Protection Law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

1. **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold.
2. **Rectify** — request correction of inaccurate data.
3. **Erase** — request deletion of data, so far as that does not conflict with a legal obligation.
4. **Restrict** — request that certain processing be limited.
5. **Port** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
6. **Object** — object to processing for marketing purposes.
7. **Withdraw consent** — at any time, for consent-based processing.

Send requests to [privasi@xyverse.my.id](mailto:privasi@xyverse.my.id). We respond within **14 working days** at the latest.

## 9. Children's data

Our service is not intended for children under 17. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you become aware that this has happened, please contact us so the data can be deleted.
