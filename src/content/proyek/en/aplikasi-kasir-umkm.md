## The challenge

Nine branches each used a different record-keeping system. The owner only learned the combined sales figure at month end, and stock discrepancies were common.

## The solution

A Flutter-based point-of-sale app running on inexpensive Android tablets. The key is the **offline mode**: transactions are still recorded when the internet drops, then sync automatically once the connection returns.

The owner's dashboard shows sales across every branch in real time, complete with low-stock warnings.

## The result

- Stock discrepancies fell from 7% to under 1%
- Combined reporting became real-time rather than monthly
- Daily till closing dropped from 30 minutes to 5
